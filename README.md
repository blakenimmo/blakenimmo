## Hi, I'm Blake 👋

Final-year **BSc Physics** student at the **University of Exeter**, interested in quantitative modelling, energy markets and data analysis.

### What I work on
- **Modelling & simulation** – merit-order price modelling, Monte Carlo methods, stellar evolution and supernova light curves (MESA + STELLA)
- **Data analysis** – time-series analysis, robust regression, cross-validation, CCD photometry, uncertainty analysis
- **Tools** – Python (pandas, NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib), REST APIs, Linux, LaTeX, Excel

### Projects
| Project | What it does |
|---|---|
| [uk-power-price-model](https://github.com/blakenimmo/uk-power-price-model) | Fundamentals model of the GB electricity price from a year of half-hourly grid data: price vs residual demand (demand − wind), out-of-sample R² 0.72 on daily prices, and the value of wind in each market regime |
| [mesa-supernova-lightcurves](https://github.com/blakenimmo/mesa-supernova-lightcurves) | Evolves 12–22 M☉ stars to core collapse with MESA, explodes them and computes Type IIP light curves with STELLA; plateau length rises from ~93 to ~136 days with mass |
| [Distance_to_XX-Cygni](https://github.com/blakenimmo/Distance_to_XX-Cygni) | Measures the distance to a pulsating star from ~300 telescope images (photometry, Lomb–Scargle period, period–luminosity relation), agreeing with Gaia DR3 |
| [monte-carlo-option-pricing](https://github.com/blakenimmo/monte-carlo-option-pricing) | Prices a European option by Monte Carlo simulation and validates it against Black–Scholes, with an N<sup>-1/2</sup> convergence study |
